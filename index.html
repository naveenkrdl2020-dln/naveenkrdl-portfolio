About

Marketing run like it reports to the CFO, not just the CMO.

I started my career as a financial analyst before moving into marketing operations, and that background never really left. I still think in reconciliations: what did we spend, what did it produce, and can I defend that number in a room. Across SaaS, IT services, and cybersecurity, mostly building for US enterprise and SMB buyers from Bangalore, that habit is what turned into a full-funnel demand generation and ABM practice.

At Athena Digital, I took over marketing for four B2B brands with no pipeline engine, no shared analytics, and no outbound motion, and built one from scratch, contributing to $9.8M in company-wide annual pipeline while running $360K or more a year in paid media. At Salesbox.ai, I ran a 13-account ABM program on deals up to $15M that influenced roughly half of total pipeline. Before that, at Zeta Global, I led a team of 38 running 200-plus omnichannel campaigns a quarter for US clients.

Since July 2026 I've been running an independent practice, auditing fragmented marketing stacks, building AI-powered GTM systems with n8n and the Claude API, and helping teams show up as the cited answer in AI search rather than just another blue link. Current clients span IT services, manufacturing, and logistics, and I'm actively building out that practice alongside AdTech, MarTech, EdTech, and Telecom, industries where the same problem shows up: good tools, no connective tissue.

I'm open to senior full-time roles, VP of Marketing, Head of Demand Generation, CMO, as well as fractional and advisory engagements, globally or remote from India.
